## Objective
Prevent reinstalling Halen on the same iOS device from restarting the local seven-day trial when the platform retains its Keychain item. Preserve existing lifetime purchases, the first trial date and local-only health data.

## Steps
1. In `lib/data/trial_start_store.dart`, reconcile the local candidate with a non-syncing, device-only iOS trial timestamp.
2. In `lib/application/entitlement_providers.dart`, resolve the trial once per database and preserve existing purchase refresh and notification semantics.
3. In `test/data/trial_start_store_test.dart`, cover migration, reinstallation, earliest-date selection and Keychain unavailability.
4. In `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`, disclose local retention of the minimal trial record.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A pre-existing trial keeps its earliest date when users upgrade or reinstall on an iOS device that retains the record.
- [ ] Secure storage failures fall back to the local database and do not block existing verified lifetime ownership.
- [ ] Trial data sent to a server or added to device backup remains zero. Android installation behavior remains unchanged.

## Verify

- `flutter analyze`

## Escalate if
- An Android cross-reinstall guarantee requires a separate decision about a durable store or account mechanism.
