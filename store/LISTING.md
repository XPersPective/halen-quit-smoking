kriz araçları — hesapsız, verin cihazında.
**Açıklama:**
Halen, sigarayı azaltıp bırakmak isteyenler için bilimsel kaynaklara dayanan
yerel bir takip uygulamasıdır.
• Kademeli azaltma planı: günlük hedefin, kayıtlarına göre uyumlanan adımlarla
  azalır; plan nasıl hesaplanır ekranda yazar.

• Üç dil: Türkçe, İngilizce, Almanca. Açık kaynak (GPL-3.0) — kod incelemeye

## EN

**Title (≤30):** Halen: Quit Smoking Tracker
**Short (≤80):** Science-based reduction plan, body-load model and crisis
tools — no account, data stays on device.
**Description:**
Halen is an offline-first, open-source (GPL-3.0) quit-smoking companion built
on published science.
• Gradual reduction plan with an explained adherence score.
• Body load: nicotine, CO and tar relative to your own baseline — model and
  limits stated on screen.
• Organ map: sourced recovery timeline (population-level, not personal).
• Crisis tools: breathing, craving log, needle-free ear acupressure labelled
  traditional.
• Money: spend, savings and lifetime cost — assumptions labelled.
• Three languages: English, Turkish, German. Encrypted local data, no
  account.
All Premium features are free for the first 7 days; the free core continues
afterwards.

Kern bleibt danach.
